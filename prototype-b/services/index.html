What we do

Three disciplines, one accountable team

Most projects need more than one of these. Because interiors, construction and landscape sit under a single contract at HST, nobody spends the project pointing at someone else.

Interior Design

Concept, space planning and 3D visualisation for villas, offices and showrooms.

Renovation & Fit-Out

Interior fit-out and refurbishment, delivered by the team that drew it.

Landscaping

Gardens, terraces and outdoor rooms designed and built for the Gulf climate.
01 — Interior Design

Design detailed enough to build from.

We start with how a space will actually be lived in or worked in, then work backwards through light, circulation, material and detail until the drawings are precise enough to build from — by the same team that will build them.

Design Consultation

We work closely with you to understand the brief, your preferences and how you actually use the space.

Concept Development & Visualisation

Ideas translated into detailed plans, mood boards and 3D renderings you can sign off before work begins.

Space Planning & Layout

Circulation, zoning and furniture layouts optimised for how the space works through the day.

Material Selection & Procurement

Stone, timber, metal and textile schedules sourced from trusted suppliers in the UAE and abroad.

Custom Furniture & Millwork

Bespoke wardrobes, vanities, reception desks and feature panelling drawn to shop-drawing standard.

Lighting & Furnishing

Layered lighting schemes plus furniture and fittings specified, procured and installed.

Interior Design in detail
02 — Renovation & Fit-Out

One contract. One team. One point of contact.

Renovation is where design either survives or quietly disappears. HST Group holds a building maintenance and technical services licence and runs its own site teams, so the drawings, the joinery, the technical works and the finishing sit under one contract.

Full Design Implementation

Execution of the approved design and drawings by our own team, from demolition through to handover.

Renovation & Construction Management

Every stage of the renovation overseen in one programme, with progress reported to you as it moves.

Interior Fit-Out

Partitions, ceilings, flooring, finishes and fixtures delivered as a single package.

Technical Services

Building services and technical works handled through HST Group's licensed technical services division.

Bespoke Joinery & Millwork

Wardrobes, kitchens, reception desks and feature panelling fabricated to the approved detail.

Annual Maintenance Contracts

Post-handover AMC cover for residential and commercial buildings, on an annual or daily basis.

Renovation & Fit-Out in detail
03 — Landscaping

Outdoor rooms, planned for the climate.

In Dubai a garden is only as good as the months it has to survive. We plan outdoor spaces around shade, planting and lighting first, so the space is genuinely usable through the cooler months and still holding together in the heat.

Landscape Design & 3D

Layouts, planting schemes and 3D visuals agreed before anything is dug.

Garden & Terrace Construction

Level changes, paving, decking, raised planters and built-in seating delivered as one package.

Pergolas & Shade Structures

Shade structures and outdoor kitchens built into the layout rather than added afterwards.

Water Features

Reflection pools, cascade walls and water features integrated with the hardscape and lighting.

Planting & Green Walls

Palms, specimen trees, green walls and ground cover chosen for heat and salinity tolerance.

Irrigation & Garden Lighting

Irrigation, drainage and low-voltage garden lighting installed with the landscape.

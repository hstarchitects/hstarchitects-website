How it works

From first visit to final snag

Programme depends on scope. We give you a written one after the first site visit.

01

Consultation & Site Survey

We visit the property, measure it, and listen to how you actually use it before proposing anything.

02

Concept & 3D Design

Mood direction, layouts and 3D renderings. You approve the finished look before anyone orders a tile.

03

Drawings & Material Schedules

Final designs, construction drawings and material selections prepared so the site team builds what was agreed.

04

Build & Fit-Out

Full design implementation by our own team, managed as one programme with progress reported to you.

05

Handover & Aftercare

Joint snagging and handover, with optional annual maintenance cover afterwards.

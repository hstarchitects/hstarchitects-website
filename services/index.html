What we do

Three disciplines, one accountable team

Most projects need more than one of these. Because interiors, construction and landscape sit under a single contract at HST, nobody spends the project pointing at someone else.

Interior Design

Concept, space planning and 3D visualisation for villas, offices and showrooms.

Renovation & Fit-Out

Turnkey refurbishment and fit-out, delivered by the team that drew it.

Landscaping

Gardens, pools, pergolas and roof terraces built for the Gulf climate.
01 — Interior Design

Design that survives the first ten years.

We design interiors that hold up on the tenth year, not just on handover day. Every HST project starts with how a space will actually be lived in or worked in, then works backwards through light, circulation, material and detail until the drawings are precise enough to build from.

Concept & Mood Direction

We open with references, materials and light studies so the direction is agreed before a single wall moves.

Space Planning & Layouts

Circulation, zoning and furniture layouts tested against how the space is used at 8am and at 8pm.

3D Visualisation

Photoreal renders and walkthroughs so you approve the finished room, not a floor plan.

Material & Finish Selection

Stone, timber, metal and textile schedules sourced from vetted UAE and European suppliers.

Joinery & Millwork Design

Bespoke wardrobes, vanities, reception desks and feature walls drawn to shop-drawing standard.

Lighting & FF&E

Layered lighting schemes plus furniture, fixtures and equipment specified, procured and installed.

02 — Renovation & Fit-Out

One contract. One team. One person answerable.

Renovation is where good design either survives or quietly disappears. We hold a building maintenance and technical services licence, run our own site teams, and manage approvals, MEP, joinery and finishing under one contract so nobody can point at anyone else when something slips.

Full Turnkey Fit-Out

Demolition, blockwork, ceilings, flooring, finishes and handover managed as one package.

Villa & Apartment Refurbishment

Structural alterations, extensions and full interior rebuilds for occupied and vacant homes.

MEP & Technical Services

HVAC, electrical, plumbing and low-current works executed by our own licensed technicians.

Bespoke Joinery & Millwork

Workshop-fabricated wardrobes, kitchens, reception desks and feature panelling.

Authority Approvals

Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence, DEWA and building-management submissions prepared and tracked.

Annual Maintenance Contracts

Post-handover AMC cover so the finish you paid for is still there in year three.

03 — Landscaping

Outdoor rooms, engineered for the heat.

In Dubai a garden is only as good as the eight months it has to survive. We design outdoor spaces around shade, breeze and irrigation first, then layer in the pool, the pergola and the planting — so the space is genuinely usable from October through May and still alive in August.

Landscape Design & 3D

Master plans, planting schemes and photoreal night visuals before anything is dug.

Swimming Pools & Water Features

Pools, plunge pools, reflection ponds and cascade walls with full filtration and plant rooms.

Pergolas & Shade Structures

Aluminium, timber and louvred structures engineered for Gulf wind and sun loads.

Hardscaping & Decking

Natural stone, porcelain, composite decking, raised planters and seating built into the level changes.

Soft Landscaping & Planting

Palms, specimen trees, green walls and ground cover chosen for salinity and heat tolerance.

Irrigation & Garden Lighting

Automated drip irrigation, drainage and low-voltage lighting on scene control.

Service 03

Landscaping in Dubai

In Dubai a garden is only as good as the eight months it has to survive. We design outdoor spaces around shade, breeze and irrigation first, then layer in the pool, the pergola and the planting — so the space is genuinely usable from October through May and still alive in August.

What is included

Outdoor rooms, engineered for the heat.

  • Shade and airflow planned before planting, not after
  • Irrigation and drainage sized for UAE summer loads
  • Night lighting designed so the garden works after sunset

Typical villa garden: 6 to 14 weeks on site depending on pool and structural works.

Landscape Design & 3D

Master plans, planting schemes and photoreal night visuals before anything is dug.

Swimming Pools & Water Features

Pools, plunge pools, reflection ponds and cascade walls with full filtration and plant rooms.

Pergolas & Shade Structures

Aluminium, timber and louvred structures engineered for Gulf wind and sun loads.

Hardscaping & Decking

Natural stone, porcelain, composite decking, raised planters and seating built into the level changes.

Soft Landscaping & Planting

Palms, specimen trees, green walls and ground cover chosen for salinity and heat tolerance.

Irrigation & Garden Lighting

Automated drip irrigation, drainage and low-voltage lighting on scene control.

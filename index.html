About us

Luxury interiors and outdoor spaces engineered to last.

HST Architects is the design arm of HST Group, a licensed UAE building maintenance and technical services company. That licence is the reason we can draw a project and then actually build it — MEP, joinery, finishes and approvals included.

Licensed & accountable

A registered UAE building maintenance and technical services company, not a design studio subcontracting the risk.

Design and build under one roof

The people who drew it are the people who build it, so design intent survives contact with the site.

Fixed scope, fixed price

A defined lump sum against a defined scope, with variations priced and approved in writing before work proceeds.

Specified for this climate

Materials, planting and systems chosen against Gulf heat, humidity and salinity rather than a European catalogue.

How we work

From first visit to final snag

Five stages, each with a deliverable you sign off before the next one starts.

01

Consultation & Site Survey

We visit the property, measure it, listen to how you actually use it, and tell you honestly what the budget will and will not buy.

02

Concept & 3D Design

Mood direction, layouts and photoreal visuals. You approve the finished room before anyone orders a tile.

03

Technical Drawings & Approvals

Construction drawings, material schedules and authority submissions prepared so the site team has no room to improvise.

04

Build & Fit-Out

Our own technicians and vetted trades execute the works under one programme with weekly progress reporting.

05

Handover & Aftercare

Joint snagging, warranties, as-built documentation and an optional annual maintenance contract.
